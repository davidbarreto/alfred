# Travel module

Remember past travels and run an upcoming trip (`travel` schema). No AI and no external API: maps links are plain URLs, coordinates are optional and user-supplied.

## Data model

- **Trips** (`travel.trips`): `name`, `start_date`/`end_date`, `travelers` (free text, one line per person: age, relation), `preferences`, `notes`, `checklist_note_id` (organizer note, SET NULL), `default_tag_names` (comma-separated *finance* tag names). **Status** (`upcoming` / `ongoing` / `past`) is derived from `local_today()`, never stored.
- **Legs** (`travel.legs`): flights, trains, buses, ferries, cars. Belong to a trip (CASCADE). Each end also has a nullable `origin_city_id` / `destination_city_id` (RESTRICT): the city of the terminal, station or address, which the text columns `origin` / `destination` (+ `*_code`) keep naming. See *Cities and countries*. `departs_at`/`arrives_at` are UTC instants stored with the IANA zone of each end (`departs_tz`/`arrives_tz`); the read model adds `departs_local`/`arrives_local`. A flight with carrier + number gets a `check_flight_url` (a plain web search, no status API).
- **Stays** (`travel.stays`): lodging periods, with a `place_id` (RESTRICT), an optional `trip_id` (SET NULL, so a trip's deletion keeps notes and grades), `timezone` of the lodging, `grade` 1-5.
- **Places** (`location.places` since migration 101, shared with the everyday places: [places.md](places.md)): independent of trips. `kind` in `PLACE_KINDS`, a required `city_id` (RESTRICT), optional `maps_url`/`latitude`/`longitude`/`address`. Travel adds tags, links, visits and stays and the derived grade (`/travel/places`); deleting is `DELETE /location/places/{id}` (409 while visits or stays hold it). `maps_link` is the user's URL, else pasted coordinates, else a name + city search. A place's country is always its city's country; there are no text columns for either.
- **Cities and countries** (`location.countries`, `location.cities`: created in `travel` by migration 091, moved to the `location` schema by migration 100; packages `features/location/countries/` and `features/location/cities/` own the tables and CRUD, `features/travel/cities/` is travel's view of them). `location` never imports travel: what travel does when a city is created or deleted (attach leg ends, refuse a city in use, drop link notes) is `TravelLocationHooks` (`travel/cities/hooks.py`, the `location.hooks.LocationHooks` protocol), passed in by `dependencies.py`. The tables: `Country(name, code)` (`code` = ISO 3166-1 alpha-2, nullable, unique when set; unique on `lower(name)`) and `City(name, country_id)` (RESTRICT; unique on `lower(name)` + country). Every city has a country and every place a city. Matching is case-insensitive on the trimmed text with no accent folding ("Lisboa" and "Lisbon" are different cities), and there is no merge: a duplicate is refused with a 409, a mistake is fixed by re-pointing places and deleting the extra city (phase 2). **Resolving names** (`CityResolver` in `location/cities/service.py`, used by place create/update and `POST /location/cities` with a country name): a country is found by its ISO `code` first (so "UK" + GB finds "United Kingdom"), else by `lower(name)` (filling in a missing code), else created as typed; a city is the one with that name in that country, else created. **Leg auto-match**: a leg end's city is the one city (in any country) whose name equals the trimmed terminal text; zero or several matches leave it null. It runs on leg create when `*_city_id` is not sent, on leg update when `origin` / `destination` changes and the city is not sent (a sent id is used as is, `null` clears it), and on city creation, which attaches every leg end with no city whose text equals the new name. Picking an airport or station does not set the city.

**City and country pages**: `GET /travel/cities/{id}` returns the city with `place_count`, its first 5 places by name (with grade), every trip that touches it (a stay or visit at one of its places, or a leg end there; newest first) and a derived `grade` (mean of its graded places' grades) with `grade_count`; `GET /travel/countries/{id}` returns its cities (each with `place_count` and grade) and trips, with `grade` the mean of its graded cities. Never stored. These are travel's read-only views; the base records are `GET /location/cities/{id}` and `GET /location/countries/{id}` (no travel figures). `PATCH /location/cities/{id}` (`name`, `country_id`; 409 when the name exists in the target country, 404 for an unknown country) and `PATCH /location/countries/{id}` (`name`, `code`, `default_tag_names`; 409 on a name or code collision; the code is uppercased, `null` clears it). `DELETE /location/cities/{id}` is a 409 naming the places, leg ends and trip homes that use it (travel's hook), `DELETE /location/countries/{id}` a 409 while it has cities; a deleted city or country takes its links (and their search notes) and a city its weather rows with it. `TripDetail` gains `cities` (`{city, role}`: `stayed` = a stay, a visited visit, or a planned visit while the trip has not ended; `passed` = only a leg end; missed-only and, on a finished trip, planned-only cities are left out) in order of first appearance, and `unmatched_leg_ends` (leg ends with no city); `GET /travel/places?city_id=&country_id=` narrows the places list. City and country pages also show **spend**: `costs` = `{linked, tagged, overall}` (the trip `Cost` shape). `linked` = transactions linked to a stay or visit at the city's places (a union, so one counts once; legs belong to the trip and are not included); `tagged` = expenses and income carrying **all** of the city's `default_tag_names` (finance tags, trimmed and lowercase), whenever dated, minus any transaction linked to a leg, stay or visit; `overall` = both. A country's `linked` covers the places of all its cities, and its `tagged` is the union of its own tags and each city's, every transaction once. Default tags are **only summed, never stamped** on transactions. A city page flags `home: true` when it is the travel settings' home city; deleting the home city, or a city that is the home of a trip, is a 409.
- **Visits** (`travel.visits`): one occurrence of going to a place (a dinner, a museum day), `planned` / `visited` / `missed`, optional `trip_id` (SET NULL) and `visit_date` / `visit_time`, `notes`, `grade` 1-5 (only a visited visit can carry one; moving it back to planned/missed clears it). Hotels use stays. A planned visit with no trip is the wishlist.
- **Tags** (`travel.tags` + `trips_tags` / `places_tags`, ORM class `TravelTag`): describe the trip or place (family, would return). Names are stored lowercase (lowercased on write, migration 089 converted the old ones), so they read the same everywhere. Not the same as `default_tag_names`.
- **Links** (`travel.links`, migration 090, ORM class `WebLink`, package `features/travel/links/`; the maps and flight URL builders live in `app/shared/utils/links.py`): a saved URL (official site, Instagram or TikTok page, video, article, booking page) owned by **exactly one trip, place, city or country** (CHECK `num_nonnulls(...) = 1`, migration 092 added `city_id` / `country_id`; CASCADE, a link has no value without its parent; a URL is unique per parent). Visits and stays carry none: a visit's details show its place's links, read-only. Generic information and booking pages go on the place (one booking page serves visits on different days). A URL tied to one booking stays a column next to its `booking_ref` (`booking_url` on legs and stays); `places.maps_url` stays the canonical map pin, a `map` link is an extra list. `kind` in `WEB_LINK_KINDS` is guessed from the domain (`kind.py`) and can be overridden. On save the backend fetches the page's title, description and image once (`integrations/link_preview/client.py`: Open Graph tags, 5 s, 1 MB, 3 redirects, public hosts only: every hop is refused when the host resolves to a private, loopback or link-local address); a failed fetch still saves the link. Instagram and TikTok answer scrapers with a login wall, so they are saved without a fetch (icon, domain and the title you type). Editing the address re-fetches; there is no refresh button. A user-typed title wins over the fetched one. A link's `notes` are embedded for `recall` (source type `web_link_note`) and dropped with the link or its parent.
- **Derived grade**: a place's `grade` is the average over its graded visits and stays, with `grade_count`; never stored.
- Enum sets (`PLACE_KINDS`, `LEG_MODES`, `VISIT_STATUSES`, `WEB_LINK_KINDS`) live in `features/travel/constants.py` and feed Pydantic, CHECK constraints and MCP descriptions.

## Times

Create bodies accept naive wall times (read in the matching `*_tz`) or values with an offset; both are stored as UTC. On update a naive value is read in the zone sent with it, else the stored zone. `arrives_at >= departs_at` and `check_out_at >= check_in_at` are enforced in the service and by CHECK constraints.

## Timeline and warnings

`GET /travel/trips` returns each trip with the sorted `cities` and `countries` of the places its stays and visits use (shown under the title in the portal list); `GET /travel/trips/locations` returns every such city and country, feeding the autocomplete of the list's City and Country filters. The places page's filters autocomplete the same way from `GET /travel/places/locations` (every place, used by a trip or not).

`GET /travel/trips/{id}` returns the trip with its legs, stays, a chronological `timeline` (leg departures, check-ins, check-outs, each with maps links: transit directions, cafes and restaurants near the hotel and at flight airports, luggage storage; a flight arrival maps the airport and links transit to the hotel even when check-in is days later) and `warnings`, computed in `features/travel/trips/timeline.py` only from data that exists:

- `long_wait_before_check_in`: a leg arrives in the stay's city more than `travel.long_wait_hours` (default 4 h; and less than 24 h) before check-in.
- `tight_connection`: consecutive legs through the same place with under `travel.tight_connection_min` (default 90 min) between them.
- `arrives_after_check_out`: a leg to the stay's city departs before check-out and arrives after it.
- `overlapping_legs`: a leg departs before the previous one arrives.
- `uncovered_night`: nights between the first arrival and the last departure that no stay covers (one warning listing the dates; a night spent on an overnight leg does not count). `no_return_leg`: the last leg ends somewhere other than where the first began (the home city is not consulted, only the legs).
- `broken_leg_chain`: a leg starts somewhere other than where the previous one ended. `overlapping_stays`: a stay begins before the previous one ends (back-to-back is fine).
- `stay_not_paid`: like `leg_not_paid`, for stays with no linked charge. `no_booking_reference`: flights, trains, buses, ferries and stays still to come with neither a booking reference nor a booking link (cars and "other" legs never get one). Both use the `unpaid_leg_days` window.
- `visits_overlap`: two timed visits of one day closer together than `travel.visit_duration_min`.
- `checklist_not_generated`: the trip starts within `travel.checklist_nudge_days` (default 60), the checklist template has items and no task titled `(<trip name>)` exists.
- On the trip page, the leg, stay and visit rows a warning points at are tinted amber with the message above them (`data-warning-row`); the timeline rows are tinted as before.
- `outside_trip_dates`: a leg, stay or dated visit (missed ones aside) on days outside the trip's start and end dates.
- `leg_not_paid`: once the trip starts within `travel.unpaid_leg_days` days (default 30, `0` = never, `/travel/settings`, edited on `/settings#travel`), every leg still to depart that has no linked transaction ("not bought yet").
- Visit checks (planned and visited, missed ones ignored; undated visits skipped, the time checks also need a time; a timed visit is read in the zone of the stay covering that day, else of the latest arrival): `visit_before_arrival`, `visit_after_departure`, `visit_overlaps_leg`, `visit_without_stay` (a stay exists on the trip but none in the place's city covers the day) and `too_many_visits` (more than `travel.max_visits_per_day`, default 4, `/travel/settings`, edited on `/settings#travel`). Computed in `trips/coverage_warnings.py`; these have `visit_id` and no timeline row.

**Home offset (27)**: each timeline item carries `home_offset` (`+1h`, `-3h`, `+5.5h` against the configured `TIMEZONE` at that instant; null when equal) and arrival rows with a difference of 3 h or more carry a one-line `sleep_hint`.

**Ideas (9)**: `TripDetail.ideas` lists planned visits with no day (the trip's own, plus wishlist visits, i.e. planned with no trip, at places in a city the trip has a stay in), grouped by stay city (`trips/ideas.py`); empty once the trip is over. The portal's *Ideas to plan* card sets `trip_id` and `visit_date` in one PATCH.

**Airport transfers**: for a flight leg the timeline offers Maps links (transit, taxi / car, walking; plain URLs, no integration) for the way *to* the departure airport (from the stay in the origin city that the traveler is leaving, else from the Home location's address on the trip's first leg; none if neither is known) and, on the arrival row, for the way *from* the arrival airport to the stay in the destination city (first check-in within 24 h after landing). Airports are searched as `<IATA> airport`. Non-flight legs keep origin → destination directions.

**Warning highlight**: every timeline item carries `warnings` (the messages of the trip warnings that point at it: the arrival and check-in rows for `long_wait_before_check_in`, the arrival and check-out rows for `arrives_after_check_out`, the departing leg for `tight_connection` / `overlapping_legs`); the portal tints those rows and prints the message under the title.

**Airport picker (portal)**: in the leg form, when the mode is `flight`, From/To are a typeahead over `web/app/static/js/airports.json` (about 8k airports with an IATA code, rows of `[iata, city, name, country, tz]`, from the `airportsdata` package, MIT). Picking a row fills the city, the IATA code and the departure/arrival time zone; all stay editable, and typing without picking saves free text. Other modes are plain text. It is portal-only: there is no airports table or API, and the backend still stores `origin`/`destination` plus nullable `origin_code`/`destination_code`. Logic in `web/app/static/js/airport-input.js`.

**Station picker (portal)**: when the leg mode is `train`, the same From/To fields search `web/app/static/js/stations.json` (about 31k European stations, rows of `[uic, name, country, tz, main, alias]` with an empty code when the dataset has none, about 1.4 MB or 290 KB gzipped, fetched once and only when a train leg is edited). Picking one fills the station name, its UIC code (in `origin_code` / `destination_code`, which hold up to 8 characters) and the time zone; accents and case are ignored when searching, and the 12 results put the best name match first, then main stations, then shorter names. Names are the local ones in the dataset; the build script adds an English alias for the cities listed in its `EXONYMS` table ("Wien Hbf" also answers to "Vienna", shown next to the country), so add a city there when its English name finds nothing. Picking a station saves its English name when it has an alias ("Vienna Hbf"), otherwise the local one: the leg-to-stay city matching (transfers, warnings) compares with the stay's city, which the city picker fills in English. The file is built by `web/scripts/build_stations.py` from the open `trainline-eu/stations` dataset (ODbL, also on Kaggle as "Train stations in Europe"), keeping suggestable stations, one per country and name (a code-less city entry such as "Helsinki" takes the UIC code of its unsuggested "Helsinki asema" / "Hbf" / "Centrale" row, when that gives exactly one code), including the rail stops at airports (Schiphol, Frankfurt Flughafen) but not the airports themselves. To add a region (for example the US from Amtrak's GTFS `stops.txt`), add a function that yields rows in the same shape and merge it in the script; the browser code is unchanged. For train legs the timeline and the map pins search Maps for `<station name> train station` (the text as typed when it already contains "station"), with the same transfer and cafe/restaurant links as airports (`terminal_query` in `trips/timeline.py`); the "leave home" reminder stays flight-only.

**City picker (portal)**: the City field of the new-place forms (stay, visit) and of the place edit form is a typeahead over the distinct cities in the same airports file; picking one fills Country with the English country name (`Intl.DisplayNames` from the file's ISO code) and a hidden `country_code` that the form posts, so the backend stores the code. Both stay editable (typing in Country drops the code), and free text still saves: the backend finds or creates the city and country by name. Logic in `airport-input.js` (`initCityInput`, `initCountryInput`).

**Leg city picker (portal)**: each end of the leg form has a city field (`travel-city-picker.js`) searching the saved cities through `GET /travel/cities/search?q=`, with a *clear* button and a last option *New city…* that opens a mini-form (name prefilled from the terminal text, country typeahead with its hidden ISO code) calling the portal's `POST /travel/cities` (which proxies `POST /location/cities`); a 409 selects the existing city instead. The id is sent only when the user picked or cleared a city, so an untouched field leaves the backend's auto-match in charge.

City matching in the timeline and warnings compares ids when the leg end has a city (`leg.destination_city_id == stay.place.city_id`), else falls back to a case-insensitive containment between the terminal text and the place's city name.

## Map pins

`GET /travel/trips/{id}/map?scope=all|planned|visited|missed` returns the trip's pins (`features/travel/trips/map_points.py`): one per place (stays, visits, and the airports of flight legs, `<IATA> airport`; a place used several times lists each use in its description). `address` is what a map importer geocodes: `lat,lng` when the place has coordinates, else name + address/city. Each pin has a `status` (`planned` / `visited` / `missed`; visited beats planned beats missed). Scopes: `planned` = still ahead (planned visits, stays not begun, flights not taken), the map to use during the trip; `visited` = visited visits, stays begun, flights taken; `missed` = visits marked missed (the last two for a history map after the trip); `all` (default) = everything, missed included, with the status in each pin. There is no Google integration: the portal's **Map pins** menu on a trip downloads `/travel/{id}/map.csv?scope=...` (columns Name, Address, Category, Status, Description), which Google My Maps imports (Create a new map, Import, position by *Address*, title by *Name*) into a saved map you can keep and share.

## Itinerary and hiding

A trip's itinerary is its visits, sorted by day then time; undated visits and those without a time come last, in creation order. Once the trip has ended, planned and missed visits are hidden (`hidden_visit_count` says how many); `GET /travel/trips/{id}?all_visits=true` returns them all.

## Transactions and cost

- **Links**: a charge is linked to the thing it paid for. Tickets and fees go on a **leg** (`travel.legs_transactions`), a hotel payment on a **stay** (`stays_transactions`), a meal or entry on a **visit** (`visits_transactions`). A place has no link of its own: three dinners at one restaurant are three visits. One transaction may be linked to several items; deleting either side removes only the link row. Transfers can't be linked. `GET/POST /travel/{legs|stays|visits}/{id}/transactions`, `DELETE .../{transaction_id}` (linking twice keeps one link).
- **Cost is derived**: expenses add, refunds (income) subtract, in `amount_eur`. A linked transaction without an EUR amount is reported as `unconverted_count` and never counted as zero. `GET /travel/trips/{id}/costs` returns the trip total (each distinct transaction once, however many items it is linked to), a breakdown by kind (legs by `mode`, stays as `lodging`, visits by the place's `kind`; a charge linked under two kinds appears under both) and the cost per leg / stay / visit. `GET /travel/places/{id}/costs` is a place's lifetime cost (its visits plus its stays) with the cost of each visit in `visits`; the place page shows the total in the header and each visit's cost on its row.
- **Charges behind a cost**: every money figure links to the transactions that add up to it. `GET /travel/trips/{id}/charges?part=linked|tagged|overall&kind=` (`kind` = a breakdown kind such as `flight` or `lodging`, only with `linked`), `GET /travel/places/{id}/charges`, `GET /travel/cities/{id}/charges?part=` and `GET /travel/countries/{id}/charges?part=` return `{transactions, cost}`, each transaction once, the cost by the same rule as the figure (so the list always adds up to it). A leg, stay or visit uses `GET /travel/{kind}/{id}/transactions`. Portal: `/travel/charges?scope=trip|place|city|country|leg|stay|visit&id=&part=&kind=&name=&back=` (`back` is only followed when it is a portal path); linked from the trip *Cost* card (overall, linked, tagged, each kind), the leg / stay / visit and place cost chips, and the city and country *Spend* card.
- **Default tags** (`trips.default_tag_names`, used for finance transactions and, later, calendar events): an **expense** dated within a trip's dates picks the trip's tags up when it is created manually (`TransactionService.create`) or imported (both statement-commit paths in `ImportService`); overlapping trips merge their tags. Income, transfers and recurring-generated rows are not stamped. Editing a trip's dates never retags anything by itself: `POST /travel/trips/{id}/apply-default-tags` (the portal's *Apply default tags* button, offered after a date change) tags the expenses already in the range, skipping those that carry the tags.
- **Tagged charges**: `GET /travel/trips/{id}/tagged-transactions` lists every expense and income carrying **all** of the trip's default tags (a charge with only some, like a shared `travel` tag from another trip, is left out) dated from `travel.charge_link_lead_days` before the trip (default 90) to `travel.charge_link_tail_days` after it (default 30; transfers never), each with `linked` (a leg, stay or visit already counts it). `cost` adds only the tagged charges that are **not** linked, so nothing is counted twice. `GET /travel/trips/{id}/costs` carries it as `tagged` (a part apart from `total`, which stays linked-only) and `overall` (`total` + `tagged`); the recap has `spend_tagged` / `spend_overall`. Unconverted tagged charges are reported, never counted as zero.
- **Unlinked charges (36)**: `GET /travel/trips/{id}/unlinked-transactions` lists only the *expenses dated within the trip* that do not carry all of its default tags and are linked to no leg, stay or visit (transfers and income never): the ones still to tag or link by hand. Tagged charges are listed apart by *Tagged charges* below, so a charge is never in both. No matching suggestions.
- **Import from calendar** (migration 086 adds the unique `calendar_event_id` to legs and stays): `GET /travel/trips/{id}/calendar-events` lists the non-recurring events of Alfred's local calendar copy whose start falls in the trip's dates (no Google call: sync the calendar first), with times shown in the event's own zone and `imported_as` / `imported_id` when already used. The portal prefills the leg or stay dialog from the event; the user picks the type and corrects the details. Creating a leg or stay with an already imported `calendar_event_id` is a 409.

## Pre-trip checklist

A template of tasks to create for a trip, stored in `core.settings` as `travel.checklist.<anchor>.<name>` where `<anchor>` is `start` or `end` (the trip date the offset counts from) and the value is a **signed** integer of days: negative = before the anchor, `0` = the anchor day, positive = after it. The task title is the name with `-` / `_` as spaces. Anchoring in the key lets a post-trip task (`end.send-photos` = `3`) ignore the trip's length. A key with an unknown anchor or no name, or a value that is not an integer, is ignored with a WARNING. Names are lowercase letters, digits, `-` and `_` and unique across both anchors, at most 50 items.

- **Defaults** are written the first time the template is read (marker row `travel.checklist_seeded`, so deleting every item does not bring them back): `start.passport-validity` -60, `start.travel-insurance` -30, `start.currency` -14, `start.sim-or-esim` -7, `start.plug-adapter` -7, `start.online-check-in` -1, `end.link-trip-charges` 2, `end.unpack-and-laundry` 1.
- **API**: `GET /travel/checklist-template` (`{items: [{anchor, name, title, offset_days}]}`), `PUT /travel/checklist-template` (`{items: [{anchor, name, offset_days}]}`, the whole template: new and changed items are written, missing ones deleted; a bad anchor or name, or a name used twice, is a 422). `POST /travel/trips/{id}/checklist` creates one normal task per item, priority `MEDIUM`, titled `"<Title> (<trip name>)"` (first letter upper-cased), due the day of `anchor date + offset` at 23:59 (moved to today if already past); an item whose task (same title) exists is skipped, so pressing it twice is safe. Tasks go through `TaskService` (Notion sync, overload and the normal reminder rules apply: a task reminds from its due day on).
- **Portal**: edited in the Travel card of `/settings` (items grouped *Around departure* / *After the trip*, offset edit, remove, add row; saved with the page's Save) and a *Generate checklist* button on the trip page.

## Calendar events for visits

A **planned** visit of a trip with a day **and** a time gets a calendar event (`visits/calendar_sync.py`, through `CalendarEventService`, so it reaches Google). The id is kept in `visits.calendar_event_id` (migration 087; unique, and an event belongs to one leg, stay or visit, else 409; a deleted event just unlinks).

- **Event**: title = place name, location = place address (else name + city), description = Maps link + visit notes, length = `travel.visit_duration_min` (default 90, `/travel/settings`, edited on `/settings#travel`), profile `normal`, organizer tags = the trip's default tags (looked up case-insensitively, created if missing), time zone = the stay covering that day, else the local zone.
- **Edits**: changing a visit's day, time, place or trip moves the linked event's **start/end** (length kept) and **location** only, never title or description. A recurring, guest-invited or all-day event is left alone. Giving a time to an unlinked planned visit creates the event; clearing the time or day leaves the event untouched.
- **Failures** (Google not authorised, API down) are logged at WARNING and never fail the visit write.
- **Delete**: `DELETE /travel/visits/{id}?delete_event=true|false` (default false). The portal asks whether to delete the event too.
- **Link / unlink** (`travel/event_links.py`): `calendar_event_id` on `POST`/`PATCH` of visits, and on `PATCH` of legs and stays (null unlinks; the event is kept). The event must exist (404) and belong to at most one leg, stay or visit (409). Linking does not rewrite the event. Alfred does not distinguish events it created from events you linked.
- **Warnings** (visits only, from Alfred's local calendar copy, so Google edits show after the next calendar sync; legs and stays are never compared): `no_calendar_event` (a planned visit dated today or later, with a time, has no event: never created, deleted or unlinked), `linked_event_mismatch` (the event starts on another day or time, compared in the event's own zone) and `linked_event_not_updated` (a mismatch on a recurring, guest-invited or all-day event, which Alfred never rewrites).
- **Repair**: `POST /travel/visits/{id}/event` with `action` = `create` (make the event), `update_event` (push the visit's day, time and place to the event) or `update_visit` (take the event's day and time); 422 with the reason when nothing changed. The portal offers these on the visit row next to *Link event* / *Unlink event* (also on legs and stays).

## During the trip: today

`GET /travel/trips/{id}/today` (`trips/today.py`) returns the traveler's calendar day (in the zone of the stay covering now, else the home zone), the next timeline step with `minutes_until_next`, today's visits and how many are still unmarked (`to_mark`). Portal: `/travel/{id}/today` (mobile-friendly card; linked from an ongoing trip) with one-tap Visited / Missed, an optional grade and a quick note appended through `PATCH /travel/visits/{id}`. Command: `travel_trip today` / `/today` (the ongoing trip when no trip is given); not in the Telegram menu.

## Around the trip: other events, weather, while away, print

- **Tagged charges**: a *Charges with the trip's tags* card (always shown, with an empty message when nothing matches, which says so when the trip has no default tags) shows the first 5 and a *See all N* link to `/travel/{id}/tagged` (every charge, with *Details*; each row says *linked* or *added to cost*; a row not linked yet has a target select and a *Link* button to tie it to a leg, stay or visit, which keeps its cost counted once but ties it to that place). The Cost card headline is the `overall` total with two parts under it (*Linked to the trip*, *Tagged, not linked*), and the card also appears when only tagged charges exist. The recap's *Spent* is the overall total, with the same two parts shown when there are tagged charges.
- **Untagged charges during the trip**: the card shows the first 5 and a *See all N* link to `/travel/{id}/unlinked`, which lists every charge with the same link controls (the card carries this name in the portal) and a *Details* link that opens the transaction (account, category, bank text, note, tags) in a modal (`/travel/transactions/{id}/detail`, a portal-only partial). The modal has an *Edit* button that swaps in a form (amount, date, type, account, merchant, category, tags with the chip input, description, note; `/travel/transactions/{id}/edit`) so a charge can be fixed on the spot, for example to add a missing default tag; saving PATCHes `/finance/transactions/{id}` through the portal (an unchanged date is not sent, so the time of day is kept) and reloads the page.
- **Warnings page**: the trip page shows a single *Worth a look · N warnings* card linking to `/travel/{id}/warnings`, and a single *N other calendar events during the trip* card linking to the same page, which lists every group (warning kinds plus other events) in foldable sections with a fold/unfold-all toggle.
- **Skipped occurrences**: a recurring event occurrence you skipped (`/eventskip`, the calendar day view) is left out of the other-events list, its count and the snoozing, because the list comes from the calendar expansion that already drops skips.
- **Other calendar events and snoozing (25, S)**: a *trip event* is a calendar event linked to a leg, stay or visit, or carrying an organizer tag that matches one of the trip's default tags (case-insensitive); a trip with no default tags relies on links alone. `GET /travel/trips/{id}/other-events` lists the others inside the trip dates (recurring ones expanded per day) with `snoozed`; the portal shows them as a warning card. With `travel.snooze_other_events` on (`/travel/settings`, default off, a switch on `/settings#travel`), `CalendarNotificationService.build_due_digest` skips the reminders of those occurrences (`other_events.py`, loaded once per run). It fails open: any error while evaluating means everything is reminded. Only reminders change; the events stay in the calendar. On the portal's warnings page each recurring row has a checkbox and a **Skip** button, with *Select all / Select none / Skip selected* for bulk: each one calls the calendar's skip-occurrence route (`POST /calendar/{id}/skip`, Alfred-only, Google is not told; undo from the calendar day view). One-off events cannot be skipped and get no controls.
- **Weather (8)**: `GET /travel/trips/{id}/forecast` gives daily min/max and precipitation per stay city (rows are keyed by `city_id`, so renaming a city changes nothing stored; a leg end with no city gets no weather), only for the days a stay in that city covers (check-in to check-out date; two separate stays skip the days between), plus the trip's two ends: the first leg's origin city on its departure day and the last leg's destination city on its arrival day (local to each leg's zone; layovers get nothing; a city that is also a stay city, or both ends being the same home city, share one row; the origin city is listed first, the destination city last, and a home city at both ends only first) (`trips/forecast*.py`, Open-Meteo): the forecast when the trip starts within 14 days (or is ongoing), else last year's same dates from the archive, Each day is saved in `travel.trip_weather` (`city_id` CASCADE, day, min/max, precipitation, `source` forecast/climate, `fetched_on`): the first request of each local day asks Open-Meteo for the days from today on and overwrites their rows, later requests that day read the table (no cron). Days that have passed are never refreshed, so they keep their last forecast; a finished trip is served from the table, and days it never saved are fetched once (the trip's own dates, `source` actual): days within 60 days back come from the forecast endpoint, which has no lag (it documents 92 days but answers with null values from about 67), older ones from the archive, which lags a few days; days the forecast endpoint has no values for are asked from the archive; a day with no values stays unsaved and is asked again, after which Open-Meteo is not called again (no card if nothing could be saved); the response has `finished` and `as_of` set, and `mode` is `actual` or `mixed` when the saved days are not all forecasts. Cities use the place's coordinates, else are geocoded by name; if Open-Meteo fails the saved days are shown (`as_of` older than today), or `available: false` with a message when there are none, and nothing else breaks. A day is `rain` from `travel.rain_mm` (default 2 mm).
- **While away (26)**: `GET /travel/trips/{id}/while-away` lists contact birthdays, active tasks with a deadline, and the recurring expenses that will be created inside the trip dates (`trips/while_away*.py`).
- **Stay essentials (31)**: stay rows in the timeline link pharmacy, supermarket and ATM searches near the stay, like the cafe and restaurant links.
- **Print (10)**: portal-only `/travel/{id}/print`, a day-by-day page (legs with flight numbers and booking refs, stays with addresses and booking refs, visits with times and Maps links; missed visits left out) with a print stylesheet for "Save as PDF".

## During the trip: moving visits and notes

- **Move visits (16)**: `POST /travel/trips/{id}/visits/move` (`from_date`, `to_date`, optional `visit_ids`, optional `only_tag`) moves the planned visits of one day to another through the normal visit update, so linked calendar events move in place; a visit that fails is returned in `failed` and the rest still move. Travel tags are free-form: `Outdoor` / `Indoor` are a convention (tag the place), not seeded. The portal offers *Move visits* on a rainy forecast day that has planned visits.
- **Quick note (17)**: `travel_visit note` / `/tnote <text>` appends `[YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM] text` (in the trip's current zone) to the latest visit of the ongoing trip whose day and time have already passed (a visit with no time counts from the start of its day; missed ones never); it re-embeds the notes. If no visit has started yet it replies with a hint and writes nothing. Not in the Telegram menu.
- **Place history (23)**: the visit and stay dialogs show, under the place picker, the place's derived grade, its tags (such as *Would return*) and a link to its history.
- **Empty days (9)**: the itinerary lists upcoming days with no visit.

## Briefings and nudges

- **Briefings (15)**: while a trip is ongoing, the morning briefing context gets a trip block (day N of M, today's legs, check-ins/outs and visits in time order, the leave-home time on the first flight's day, spend so far from linked costs) and the evening digest says how many of today's visits are still to be marked visited or missed (`features/travel/briefing.py`). A failing trip block is logged and left out; it never fails the briefing.
- **Grade nudge (39)**: `TravelReminderService` adds `travel_grade_nudge` one and seven days after `end_date` when the recap still has visited visits or stays without a grade or notes: one reminder per day, linking `/travel/{id}/recap`. Trips that ended up to 7 days ago are loaded for it.

## After the trip: recap and search

`GET /travel/trips/{id}/recap` (portal `/travel/{id}/recap`, command `travel_trip recap`) builds the recap from the trip with **all** its visits (hidden ones included) and its costs, in `features/travel/recap/builder.py`:

- **Numbers**: days, legs, stays, visits visited / missed / never done, spend (total, by kind, with the unconverted count), the cities and countries of its stays and of visits that actually happened.
- **To complete**: every visited visit and every stay still lacking a grade and/or notes (blank notes count as missing), by date. Planned and missed visits are never asked for a grade.
- **Highlights**: visits and stays graded 4 or 5, best first.

**Fixing the gaps**: each *Still to write down* row has an *Edit* link to `/travel/{trip}?all_visits=1&edit=visit-<id>` (or `stay-<id>`), which opens that visit's or stay's edit dialog on the trip page.

Search and filters: trips by `city` / `country` (a stay or visit in a place there), as well as status, tag, name and date range; places by kind, city, country, tag, minimum grade, `ungraded` (no grade from any visit or stay), visit status, `no_trip`, `has_visits` and `active`.

## Home

Trips start from the **Home place** (`location.places`, kind `home`): its address is the home address (migration 095 moved the old `organizer.home_address` setting there) and its city is the home city (migration 102 removed the old `travel.home_city_id` setting). `GET /travel/settings` returns them as `home_address`, `home_city_id` / `home_city`, plus `home` ("<address>, <city>") and `home_place_id`; `PUT /travel/settings` refuses `home`, `home_address` and `home_city_id` with a 422 that points to `/location/places`, and `/settings#travel` shows one read-only *Home* row linking to the Home place (`/locations/home`). Change them on the Home place (portal edit form: Address and City).

Changing a home value must not rewrite how a past trip was planned, so **before** `PUT /travel/settings` saves a changed `airport_buffer_min` or `home_to_airport_min`, and before location's `PlaceService` saves a change of Home's address or city (an edit, a cleared address, Home becoming another kind, or Home deleted), the *old* value is copied into every trip with `start_date <= today` whose own column (`trips.home_city_id` / `home_address` / `airport_buffer_min` / `home_to_airport_min`, migration 093) is NULL. The place service gets that call as a callable from `dependencies.py` (`HomeSnapshot`: one `TripRepository.snapshot_home_value` per changed value), so `location` does not import travel. A value that was never set is not copied, and upcoming trips keep following the current one. This is logged at INFO with the field name and trip count, never the address.

**Effective home** (`trips/home.py`, `effective_home(trip, settings)`): each value is the trip's column when not NULL, else the setting. The timeline's first airport transfer, the leave-home time, the reminders and the `home` role of a trip's cities all use it. `TripDetail.home` = `{city, address, airport_buffer_min, home_to_airport_min, from_trip}`; `from_trip` is true when the trip has any value of its own. A trip's own city and address are editable on the trip form (*Home for this trip*; blank follows the Home place again); the two minute values are only on `PATCH /travel/trips/{id}` (0-600). A trip's home city has the role `home` in `TripDetail.cities` when the trip touches it (it wins over `stayed` and `passed`) and the trip page lists it first with a home badge. Portal: the home city is the City field of the Home place's edit form; `/travel` shows a one-line summary of the travel settings with a link to `/settings#travel`.

## Leave-home time and reminders

If a trip's first leg is a flight, the trip detail has `leave_home_at` (departure minus `travel.airport_buffer_min` minus `travel.home_to_airport_min`, both runtime settings in `core.settings`) and a `leave_home` timeline item. Reminders (trip starts, online check-in, time to leave, check-out day) are described in [reminders-overload.md](reminders-overload.md#travel-reminders).

## API

Links: `GET`/`POST /travel/trips/{id}/links`, `GET`/`POST /travel/places/{id}/links`, `GET`/`POST /travel/cities/{id}/links`, `GET`/`POST /travel/countries/{id}/links`, `GET`/`PATCH`/`DELETE /travel/links/{id}` (409 for a URL already saved on that parent, 422 for a non-http address or an unknown kind).

Cities (the `location` API): `GET /location/countries`, `GET /location/countries/{id}`, `GET /location/cities?country_id=&q=` (case-insensitive substring, no pagination), `GET /location/cities/{id}`, `POST /location/cities` with `{name, country_id}` or `{name, country, country_code?}` (exactly one of the two country forms, else 422; 404 for an unknown `country_id`; 409 when the city exists in that country). Places take `city_id`, or `city` + `country` (+ `country_code`), on create (one form required, else 422) and either form on update (a lone `city` or `country` keeps the other from the place's current city; an unknown `city_id` is a 404); `PlaceRead.city` is a nested `{id, name, country: {id, name, code}}`. Legs take `origin_city_id` / `destination_city_id` and read back `origin_city` / `destination_city` (`CityRef` or null).

`/travel/trips` (list filters: `status` = current (default) / upcoming / ongoing / past / all, repeated `tag`, `q`, `city`, `country`, `date_from`, `date_to`, `limit`, `offset`), `/travel/legs`, `/travel/stays`, `/travel/visits` (filters `trip_id`, `place_id`, `status`, `date_from`, `date_to`, `no_trip`), `/travel/places` (filters `q`, `kind`, `city`, `country`, repeated `tag`, `min_grade`, `ungraded`, `status` = has a visit with it, `no_trip` = no visit or stay belongs to a trip, `has_visits` = has a visit of any status or a stay (the *Visited* view), `active`; `GET /{id}` returns the history; deleting a place with visits or stays is a 409), `/travel/settings` (GET/PUT), `/travel/trips/{id}/costs`, `/travel/trips/{id}/recap`, `/travel/trips/{id}/map`, `/travel/trips/{id}/calendar-events`, `/travel/{kind}/{id}/transactions`, `/travel/tags` (usage counts, rename, delete removes only link rows). Postman: `postman/travel.collection.json`.

## Portal

**Cities and countries:** `/travel/cities` (every country with its cities, a *New city* button), `/travel/cities/{id}` (flag, country link, grade, edit/delete, a Trips card and a Places card capped at 5 with *See all* links to `/travel/cities/{id}/trips` and `/travel/places?city_id=`, and a Links card), `/travel/countries/{id}` (cities and trips capped at 5, with `/travel/countries/{id}/cities` and `/travel/countries/{id}/trips`, and a Links card), and the links pages `/travel/cities/{id}/links`, `/travel/countries/{id}/links`. They are reached from a *Cities* card on the trip page (stayed cities as chips, *Passed through* below, a hint when leg ends have no city), from the place page header, the places list, visit rows and the stays' city, and from *Cities* on `/travel`. Portal writes go through `/travel/cities/{id}` and `/travel/countries/{id}` (proxied to `/location/...`) and `/travel/{cities,countries}/{id}/links`.

**Links of the cities and countries (read-only):** under the trip's own Links card, one group per city (the trip's home city first, then each stayed city in trip order; passed-through cities get none) and then one per country of those cities in order of first appearance; the place page shows its city's links. Each group is capped at 5 with *See all N links* to the owner's links page, empty groups are hidden and a URL already shown higher on the page is not repeated. No edit or delete there: change them on the city or country page.

**Links:** a *Links* card on the trip page and the place page (kind icon, title or domain, domain and your note on one line each; an *Add link* dialog; edit and delete per row), capped at 5 with a *See all N links* page (`/travel/{id}/links`, `/travel/places/{id}/links`) that adds the thumbnail and the fetched description. The visit details dialog lists the place's links, fetched on demand from `/travel/web-links/place/{id}` (the visit row and the trip page never carry them). Portal writes go through `/travel/trips/{id}/links`, `/travel/places/{id}/links` and `/travel/web-links/{id}`.

`/travel`: a one-line settings summary linking to `/settings#travel` (where the pre-trip checklist is edited too) and a trips list with status, text, date-range and tag filters (HTMX partial `/travel/list`, `limit + 1` pagination). `/travel/{id}`: header with inline edit (including a *Checklist note* picker over the 200 most recent active notes; the linked note shows as a collapsed title/content line), warnings, then (side by side from the `lg` breakpoint, stacked on mobile) the day-grouped timeline, cut to its first 8 steps with a *See all N steps* link to `/travel/{id}/timeline` (the full timeline on its own page), and the itinerary (visits by day, mark visited/missed, add/edit/delete, a toggle for hidden ones), legs and stays with add/edit/delete dialogs, a cost card and per-item cost chips, a linked-transactions dialog on every leg, stay and visit, *Import from calendar*, *Apply default tags* and a *Recap* link; `/travel/places` (filterable list, with a *New place* form to create a place outside any trip) and `/travel/places/{id}` (edit, tags, derived grade, visit and stay history, plan a visit); a new hotel can be created inline in the stay dialog. Writes proxy through `/travel/{trips,legs,stays,places,visits,cities}` (plus `GET /travel/cities/search` for the leg city picker), `/travel/links/{kind}/{id}` and `/travel/transactions/search`.

## Assistant layer

- **Commands** (handler `app/assistant/commands/handlers/travel.py`, one MCP tool per group): `travel_trip` (list, get, add, update, delete, costs, recap, apply_tags), `travel_leg` / `travel_stay` / `travel_visit` (add, update, delete, link, unlink; visits also `visited` / `missed` and `list`). Places themselves (add, list `--view visited`, show with grade, visits and stays, tags, grade) are the `place` command, shared with everyday places ([places.md](places.md#command-and-chat)). Telegram aliases follow the pattern `/trips`, `/trip`, `/tripadd`, `/legadd`, `/stayadd`, `/visitadd`, `/visits`, `/visited`, `/missed`, `/leglink`, ...; only `/trips`, `/trip` and `/visited` are in the Telegram menu, the rest are typed.
- **Links** (`travel_link`: `add`, `list`, `update`, `delete`, `suggest`; aliases `/linkadd`, `/links`, `/linkupdate`, `/linkdelete`, `/linksuggest`): the owner is one of `--trip`, `--place`, `--city`, `--country`, each an id or a name, and exactly one is required (a `--country` next to a `--city` only narrows the city). A city name that exists in several countries is refused with the list of countries, never guessed. `suggest <trip>` returns the trip's home and stayed cities and their countries with the addresses already saved: **Alfred never searches the web**, so Claude searches for official sites and tips, shows the candidates, and saves only the ones the user picks with `add`.
- **References by name**: `trip get` takes an id, a name fragment (a current trip first, then the latest past one) or `next` (the soonest upcoming or ongoing trip); A place is an id or a name, by the rule of [places.md](places.md) (everyday places and the home city first, else the places in `--city`; a name in several cities is a 409). `visit add` with a name nobody has creates a place of `--kind` (default `other`) in `--city` and `--country`, or in the home city when neither is given; `stay add --hotel` does the same with kind `hotel`.
- **Times**: legs need both zones (`--departs-tz`, `--arrives-tz`); a missing zone is a 400 that names the field. An empty value on an update clears the field.
- **Natural language**: intents `travel_trip.list` and `travel_trip.get` (extraction schema `TravelTripGetArgs`, field `trip`, or `next`); both are reads. Writes are slash/MCP only.
- **Recall**: the `notes` columns of trips, legs, stays, visits and places are embedded (`trip_note`, `leg_note`, `stay_note`, `visit_note`, `place_note`; `app/features/travel/notes_index.py`) with a label prefix such as `Stay at Hotel Example: ...`, and `recall` searches them. The index follows create / edit / clear / delete; deleting a trip also drops its legs' embeddings.
